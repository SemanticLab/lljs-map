About the atlas

This atlas maps places named in the Linking Lost Jazz Shrines oral histories. Select a shape or an index entry to read about a place and the people associated with it.

The map

Cylinders mark jazz shrines, cubes mark organizations, and pyramids mark institutions. Zoom in to see larger shapes and richer colors. Larger markers within each category indicate greater connectedness in the interview collection. Count the notes rising together to read a site's score range. Hover over a marker or open its record for the exact score. Links to people, groups, and places supply half the score. The number of interviews and the number of mentions each supply a quarter.

The records

The Communities layer groups shrines by the people named in their records. Select a colored circle or a group in the map key to explore its people. The five groups include 73 shrines with shared affiliations. The remaining shrines have no recorded overlap. Group totals include unmapped locations.

The collection includes venues, schools, and organizations from several decades. Summaries and activity dates are model-assisted readings of interview passages and Wikibase statements. Each record links to its source.
